## 1. Overview

BlazeDrop ("the App", "we", "us") is a local file-transfer utility for Android and iOS. It lets you send and receive files directly between devices on the same local network, either device-to-device (P2P Mode) or through a temporary local web server hosted on your own device (Server Mode).

**The short version:** BlazeDrop does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with us or with any third party. There are no accounts, no cloud storage, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs. Files you transfer are sent directly between your devices over your local network and never pass through our systems.

This policy explains what the App does on your device, which permissions it requests, and the choices you have.

## 2. Information We Do **Not** Collect

We do not collect or receive any of the following:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account credentials (the App has no sign-up or login).
- Precise or approximate location, GPS coordinates, or location history.
- The contents of your files, photos, videos, documents, or messages.
- Your contacts, calendar, or address book.
- Advertising identifiers or any device identifier used for tracking.
- Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
- Any data that is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

BlazeDrop has no backend service that stores your data. Nothing you do in the App is uploaded to us.

## 3. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To function, the App stores a small amount of data **only on your device**. This data is not transmitted to us:

| Data | Purpose | Where it is stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device name | Identifies your device to nearby peers during discovery | Local app storage |
| Random node identifier (UUID) | Distinguishes devices on the network | Local app storage |
| App settings (e.g. discovery, session timeout, theme) | Remembers your preferences | Local app storage |
| Transfer history | Shows your recent transfers (file names, sizes, direction, status, timestamps) | Local app storage |
| Files you send or receive | Performs the transfer you requested | Local app storage (received files folder) |
| Server Mode staging files | Temporarily holds files uploaded by guests or published by you while the local server is running | Local staging folder (cleared on each server start) |

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the transfer history, resetting the App's settings, or uninstalling the App.

## 4. How Transfers Work

**P2P Mode.** Both devices run BlazeDrop. Your device discovers nearby peers over the local network (Wi-Fi Direct / local discovery services) and opens a direct connection to the device you select. Files travel directly between the two devices on your local network.

**Server Mode.** The App starts a temporary web server **on your own device**. Guests on the same local network connect to it with a browser (by scanning a QR code or entering the address shown). Files uploaded by guests are stored on your device; files you publish are served from your device.

In both modes, transfers occur within your local network. **We do not relay, store, monitor, or have access to the files you transfer.** However, anything you send can be saved, copied, or forwarded by the receiving party — please only transfer files to devices and people you trust.

## 5. Permissions and Why They Are Requested

The App requests the following permissions. Each is used only for the stated purpose, and none is used to collect data about you.

- **Local network / Nearby Wi-Fi devices** — to discover nearby devices and establish direct connections.
- **Location (Android)** — Android requires the location permission for Wi-Fi and nearby-device scanning. The App uses it **only** to enable device discovery; it does not access or store your geographic location.
- **Photos, media, and files** — to let you select files to send, save files you receive, and publish files from the host device.
- **Notifications** — to show transfer progress and completion.
- **Internet** — required by the operating system to open local network sockets and run the local server. The App does not use it to send your data to us.

You can review or revoke permissions at any time in your device settings. Revoking required permissions may prevent parts of the App from working.
